# Academic Report Structure

The complete adaptable manuscript is maintained in [`academic-report.md`](academic-report.md).
This page is the concise formatting checklist for transferring that verified content into the
institution's template.

## Preliminary pages

Title page, declaration, certification, dedication, acknowledgements, abstract, contents, lists of figures, tables, and abbreviations will follow the institution's supplied format.

## Chapter One: Introduction

Background, problem statement, aim, objectives, research questions, significance, scope, limitations, and definition of terms.

## Chapter Two: Literature Review

Eye-care access and education, clinical decision-support systems, expert systems, rule-based reasoning, forward chaining, related systems, ethical constraints, and identified research gap.

## Chapter Three: Methodology and System Design

Requirements elicitation, chosen development process, feasibility, architecture, data model, knowledge representation, inference design, security and privacy, interface design, and evaluation plan.

## Chapter Four: Implementation, Testing, and Results

Development environment, subsystem implementation, representative rules and traces, testing methods, results, usability and accessibility evidence, security evaluation, and discussion.

## Chapter Five: Summary and Conclusion

Summary, findings, contribution, limitations, conclusion, and recommendations for future work.

## References and appendices

Use one consistent citation style once the institution confirms it. Appendices will include selected source code, schemas, test evidence, user documentation, screenshots, and project-management evidence.

## Evidence rule

Only accepted implementation and test evidence may be presented as a result. The report must
continue to distinguish software conformance from clinical validation and must not invent
participants, expert review, diagnostic accuracy, or patient outcomes.
